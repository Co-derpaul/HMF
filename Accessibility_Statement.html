At HMF, we are committed to making our website accessible to everyone, including individuals with disabilities. We are dedicated to providing an inclusive online experience and have taken steps to ensure that our website complies with accessility standards. If you have any questions or need assistance with accessing our website, please contact us.

Accessibility
Statement

This statement reflects our ongoing efforts to enhance the accessibility of our website. We strive to make our site inclusive and user-friendly for all individuals, including those with disabilities.

What web
accessibility is

Web accessibility ensures that people with disabilities can perceive, understand, navigate, and interact with the web, providing equal access and opportunity to everyone.

Accessibility
adjustments on this site

Our website has been developed in accordance with web accessibility guidelines to ensure a seamless browsing experience for individuals using assistive technologies. We have implemented various adjustments to enhance accessibility:

- Utilized accessibility tools to identify and address potential issues
- Structured the site's content to facilitate easy navigation
- Provided descriptive text for all images
- Ensured color contrast for readability
- Minimized the use of animations
- Ensured accessibility of multimedia content

We are fully committed to
meeting the accessibility
standards and continuously
improving the user experience
for all visitors.

In some cases, third-party content may impact the accessibility of certain pages on our site. We are actively working to address these issues and enhance the accessibility of all content.

Accessibility arrangements
in the organization

HMF is dedicated to fostering accessibility not only on our website but also in our physical locations. We have implemented various accessibility arrangements in our offices and branches to ensure that everyone can access our services and facilities with ease. Our commitment to accessibility extends to all aspects of our organization.
